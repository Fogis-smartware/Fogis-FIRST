All Smartware products undergo rigorous testing to meet international safety, environmental, and performance standards.云智迈科技全系产品均经过严格测试，符合国际安全、环保与性能标准。

CE

European Conformity欧盟合规

Meets EU safety, health, and environmental protection requirements for products sold within the European Economic Area.满足欧洲经济区内销售产品的安全、健康和环保要求。
